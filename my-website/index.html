SIGNALIS

GENRE Survival Horror
YEAR 2022
PLATFORM PC / Console
DEV/PUB rose-engine / Balor Games

Signalis is a survival-horror game created by the beautiful folks at rose-engine and published by Balor Games (as of 2026).

Gameplay is HEAVILY reminiscent of resident evil and silent hill so if you like those you'll like that except with a twist (i love twists) its a 2.5D perspective. the combat is what you'd expect from this type of game with a few puzzles here and there.

the artwork are BEAUTIFULLY made and the story is packed with emotion and the heavy feeling of loss (I wont spoil here)

I could genuinely go on and on about the beauty of this game and what makes it so perfect, but I believe going into this with an open mind and no spoilers is the way to go.

this is a MUST PLAY for anybody and will be an experience that sticks with you for a while

VA11HALLA

GENRE Visual Novel
YEAR 2016
PLATFORM PC
DEV/PUB Sukeban Games / PLAYISM

one of my personal favorite games ever.

va11halla (or VA-11 Hall-A: Cyberpunk Bartender Action) is (as its full legal name suggests) a cyberpunk bartending experience.

it is a cozy visual novel with an emphasis on mixing drinks for patrons to steer the conversation in different ways, thus replacing "dialogue choices" with a drinks menu instead. very clever 10/10 for that alone

every ounce of this game oozes charm, style and personality and every character that walks into the bar has that on FULL DISPLAY

the art is very well done and Japanese pc-98 inspired with a hint of cyberpunk dystopia. the soundtrack (which you get to pick before the start of each shift) is very well done and does a good job of bringing home that "cozy bar" feel

This game is a must-play for those into visual novels or those looking for a cozy experience and to immerse themselves in a new experience.

p.s. this game genuinely changed my outlook on life when I played it at a decently low point in my life and I hope it does the same for you if you seek to play it :)

PERIPETEIA

GENRE Immersive Sim
YEAR 2023
PLATFORM PC
DEV/PUB Ninth Exodus

peripeteia (or simply "Peri" as i will call it here) is a first-and-third-person role-playing stealth game in a dystopian cyberpunk Poland. It is developed and published by Ninth Exodus.

Peri plays similar to an immersive sim like those made around the time of the old DEUS EX games. its a niche genre for a lot of people but I think many people can find fun in it.

there's tarkov-like inventory management (grid-based inventory with ammo and mag rationing) and gunplay combined with RPG elements of finding various ways to complete a level.

one of my favorite aspects is how you can learn and explore levels to go from taking hours to complete them to a mere few minutes. the game allows many ways to solve problems as most of the environment is parkour-able and interactable.

the artistic design is stunning and it nails its dystopian cyberpunk aesthetic perfectly. the music is also full of banger tracks.

my only issue with Peri is that it is in early access currently and it shows. the game has a fair share of bugs and glitches and needs some revamping on the AI interaction, both quests and enemies, but otherwise, there's great potential here.

PICAYUNE DREAMS

GENRE Roguelite / Bullet Hell
YEAR 2023
PLATFORM PC
DEV/PUB Stepford, Andyland, milkypossum / 2 Left Thumbs

Picayune Dreams is a combination of roguelike "upgrade per run" games similar to vampire survivors with bullet-hell gameplay. it is developed by Stepford, Andyland, and milkypossum and published by 2 Left Thumbs.

I'm not going to lie to you, this game is very strange in terms of literally everything but in the best way possible. the game is very psychedelic and will make no sense (narratively) most of the time. HOWEVER, the gameplay loop is very refreshing and addicting. it does more than just be a "vampire survivors clone" in an already pretty saturated market.

the upgrades are fun to try out. the bosses are fun to get good at. the roguelite progression keeps things unique every run and it will demand you get good or keep dying trying.

the visuals in this game are so beautiful and truly special. Andyland did an amazing job delivering a dream-like and glitchy aesthetic and wrapped it up with a nice bow.

the music is so so good, genuinely love listening to this OST.

please try this game if you are looking for a bullethell game to sink some time into.

STUDIO SYSTEM

GENRE Survival Horror
YEAR 2025
PLATFORM PC
DEV/PUB alreti circle

Studio System (or better known as Studio System : Guardian Angel) is an interesting take on the fixed-camera survival horror genre. it was developed and published by alreti circle.

this game is mostly for those that don't mind fixed camera perspectives i.e. old resident evil style. during my playthrough of this game, i was immediately hooked on the premise and surrealist nature of the environments and characters. it reminded me very heavily of the 1999 cult classic anime Serial Experiments Lain and i believe that is where most of the inspiration for this game comes from, everything from character interactions to pieces of the soundtrack so bonus points for that :)

the character sprites and 3d models for friends and monsters are also really well done and i enjoyed them a lot as i've never seen models look as unique as this in a game.

now this game isnt without its flaws unfortunately. the gameplay has a bit of a jank to it. nothing too horrible or unplayable but just a *jank* that i could feel. i think this game is worth a fair shot and its also not too long to play through and rewards multiple endings.
